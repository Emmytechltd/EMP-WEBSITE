About Our Set

EMP 2015 Set – A Legacy of Excellence

Our History

The EMP 2015 Set is a distinguished cohort of the Educational Management and Policy Department at the Umunze Branch of Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Awka. Our journey began in 2011 when we were admitted as fresh students, embarking on a transformative academic experience that would shape our professional lives and create bonds that would last a lifetime.

During our four years on campus, we navigated rigorous coursework, challenging projects, and personal growth. From matriculation to graduation in 2015, every moment was filled with memorable experiences – late-night study sessions, collaborative research projects, cultural activities, and the unforgettable moments shared with our peers and mentors.

Since our graduation, we have dispersed across Nigeria and beyond, pursuing diverse careers in education administration, policy development, consultancy, and various other fields. Yet, the EMP 2015 spirit continues to unite us, fostering a community dedicated to excellence and mutual support.

Our Department

Educational Management and Policy at Nnamdi Azikiwe University

Educational Management and Policy (EMP) Department

The Educational Management and Policy Department is a leading academic unit at Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Awka, dedicated to training professionals in educational administration, management, and policy formulation.

The department offers comprehensive programs that equip students with knowledge and skills in:

  • Educational Administration and Management
  • Educational Policy Development and Implementation
  • School Leadership and Governance
  • Educational Planning and Resource Management
  • Research Methods in Education
  • Curriculum Development and Evaluation

Our graduates are equipped to lead educational institutions, develop policies, and contribute to the improvement of the education sector at local, national, and international levels.
