Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I join the EMP 2015 alumni association?

If you're a member of the 2015 EMP set, you're already part of our community! Contact us via email or WhatsApp to be added to our official group and receive updates.

How often do we have reunions?

We organize reunions and get-togethers periodically. Subscribe to our WhatsApp group and follow our social media for announcements about upcoming events.

Can I update my profile information?

Yes! Contact our executive members with your updated information (name, current role, location, contact details) and we'll update our members page.

How can I contribute to the website?

You can contribute by sharing photos, news, or information about yourself or fellow alumni. Send your contributions via email or contact the webmaster.

Is there a WhatsApp group for our set?

Yes! Our WhatsApp group is active for sharing updates, organizing events, and staying connected. Contact the group admins for an invitation link.

Are there any dues or fees?

Our alumni association is primarily voluntary. Any contributions for events or activities will be communicated to members well in advance.
